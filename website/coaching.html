I already have hundreds of hours of coaching experience, working with players across a wide range of skill levels.

What We Can Work On

♥ Game Sense ♥

Improve your understanding of when to fight, when to disengage, when to rotate, and how to read the overall flow of a match.

♥ Positioning ♥

Work on where you should be during fights, how to use cover and elevation, and how to avoid unnecessary exposure.

♥ Communication ♥

Improve callouts, information sharing, team coordination, and how to communicate clearly without overloading your teammates.

♥ Decision-Making ♥

Review the choices you make during fights, rotations, cashouts, revives, and other high-pressure situations.

♥ Loadouts & Roles ♥

Discuss weapons, gadgets, specializations, team roles, and how your loadout fits your preferred playstyle.

♥ Teamplay ♥

Coaching can also focus on full-team coordination, spacing, role balance, target focus, and how the team works together as a unit.

Session Options

Sessions can be structured around what helps you most.

♥ Live Gameplay Coaching ♥

I watch you play and provide feedback during or immediately after gameplay.

This works well for identifying habits, positioning issues, and decision-making in real situations.

♥ VOD Review ♥

We review recorded gameplay together and pause to discuss specific decisions, mistakes, strengths, and alternative options.

♥ Strategy Discussion ♥

Sessions can focus more heavily on theory, team strategy, loadouts, map approach, or questions you want to discuss in depth.

♥ Mixed Session ♥

We can combine live coaching, VOD review, and strategy discussion within the same session.

Coaching FAQ

Quick answers about coaching sessions.

Can you coach a full team?

Yes.

The session price is the same whether I'm coaching one player or a full team.

Do I need recorded gameplay?

No.

VOD review is only one option. We can also do live coaching or focus on strategy and questions.

What skill levels do you coach?

Coaching can be useful for a wide range of players, from people learning fundamentals to more experienced players looking to improve specific habits or team coordination.

Can we focus on one specific problem?

Absolutely.

If you already know what you want to work on, we can focus the entire session around that area.

Can I ask questions during the session?

Yes.

Questions and discussion are encouraged. The goal is not just to tell you what went wrong, but to help you understand why and what you can do differently.
